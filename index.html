About me

A programmer with a research mindset

I am a software professional currently working as a Programmer at the Investment Corporation of Bangladesh (ICB), a state-owned investment bank under the Financial Institutions Division of the Ministry of Finance. I joined ICB in 2018 as an Assistant Programmer and have since supported the organisation's core systems and data-center operations.

Before public service, I built enterprise web applications on the national e-Government Procurement (e-GP) platform at Dohatec New Media, and taught undergraduate computer science as a Lecturer at City University.

Alongside my work I completed an M.Sc. (Eng.) in ICT at BUET, where my thesis on Bangla text sentiment analysis led to three peer-reviewed journal papers combining lexicon-based methods with supervised machine learning, deep learning and graph neural networks.
